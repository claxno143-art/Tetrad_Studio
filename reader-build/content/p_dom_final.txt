sum: Baba Yaga lives in a hut on chicken legs at the edge of a dense forest, flies in a mortar steered by a pestle and sweeps her tracks with a broom. Folklorists see her as guardian of the border between the living and the dead, hence her bone leg. She can devour guests or feed and guide the hero, depending on whether he is respectful or insolent.
