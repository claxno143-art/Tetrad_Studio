sum: Suspension connects wheels to the body and damps bumps: springs take the hits and shocks stop the body bouncing. Worn bushings, ball joints and tie rod ends cause knocks, steering play and uneven tire wear. After suspension work, alignment must be set, and lowering a car improves handling but can cost you an oil pan on rough roads.
