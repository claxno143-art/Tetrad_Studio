1| People said that a new face had appeared on the [embankment]{набережной}: a lady with a little dog.
2| Dmitri Dmitrich Gurov, who had [spent]{проживший} two weeks in Yalta by then and had grown used to the place, had also begun to take an interest in new faces.
3| Sitting in the [pavilion]{павильоне} at Vernet's, he saw a young lady walk along the embankment, a short blonde in a [beret]{берете}; a white [spitz]{шпиц} was running after her.
4| And after that he kept running into her in the town garden and on the [square]{сквере} [several times]{по нескольку раз} a day.
5| She walked alone, always in the same beret, with the white spitz; nobody knew who she was, and people simply called her "the lady with the little dog."
note: новое лицо | a new face, a newcomer | «Лицо» here means a person, as in English "a new face in town." It also has a legal sense: «юридическое лицо», a legal entity.
note: по нескольку раз | several times each | «По» with «нескольку» (dative of «несколько») gives a distributive sense: several times a day, every day.
q: main | What is this opening mainly about? | *A newcomer at a seaside resort catches Gurov's attention | Gurov arrives in Yalta and looks for work | A lady loses her dog on the embankment | Gurov describes his family life
q: detail | How long had Gurov been in Yalta? | One day | *Two weeks | Since spring | Several years
q: inference | What does it suggest that people call her only "the lady with the little dog"? | *She keeps to herself, so no one knows her name | She is famous in Yalta | She is Gurov's wife | She works at the pavilion
q: tone | What is the mood of the passage? | Tense and frightening | *Calm and idle, with quiet curiosity | Angry and bitter | Mournful
sum: In Yalta, the resort crowd notices a newcomer, a young lady who walks alone with a white spitz. Gurov, bored after two weeks there, keeps seeing her and becomes curious about her.
