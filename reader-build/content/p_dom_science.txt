ru: То, что зашифровано открытым ключом, может расшифровать лишь обладатель закрытого. || What is encrypted with the public key can be decrypted only by the [holder]{обладатель} of the private one.

ru: Электронная подпись работает в обратном направлении: документ подписывается закрытым ключом, а проверить подлинность подписи может любой, у кого есть открытый. || A [digital signature]{электронная подпись} works the other way round: a document is signed with the private key, and anyone who has the public key can [verify]{проверить} the signature's [authenticity]{подлинность}.
ru: Специалисты опасаются, что появление достаточно мощных квантовых компьютеров сделает часть нынешних алгоритмов уязвимыми, и уже разрабатывают постквантовые стандарты. || [Experts]{специалисты} fear that the [arrival]{появление} of sufficiently powerful [quantum]{квантовых} computers will make some of today's [algorithms]{алгоритмов} [vulnerable]{уязвимыми}, and are already developing [post-quantum]{постквантовые} standards.
note: стойкость | strength (of a cipher) | Literally steadfastness or durability. In cryptography it's resistance to cracking: «криптостойкость».
note: простые множители | prime factors | «Простое число» is a prime number. «Простой» here means prime, not "simple."
q: main | What problem does public-key cryptography solve? | Making data smaller | *Exchanging keys securely, since only the private key decrypts | Speeding up the internet | Storing passwords
q: detail | How does a digital signature work? | It is encrypted with the public key | *The document is signed with the private key and checked with the public one | It uses no keys | It is handwritten
q: inference | Why are quantum computers a threat? | They use more electricity | *They might solve the hard math problems these ciphers rely on | They can't run old software | They make keys longer
sum: Symmetric ciphers use one key, so exchanging it safely is hard. Public-key cryptography uses a pair: anyone can encrypt with the public key, only the private key decrypts, and signatures work in reverse. Security rests on hard math such as factoring, which future quantum computers may break, so post-quantum standards are being developed.
